# Suspicions of medical research led by Columbia University and / or their attached state funded New York Psychiatric Institute

The purpose of this document is to outline my suspicions, in a timeline format, of unethical, non consensual (lacking informed consent) medical research led by a combination of Columbia University and it's joint venture of the state funded New York State Psychiatric Institute.

### June 2022 - Therapist irrationally recommends Columbia University Schizophrenia Research Center

In an appointment with MTAT (my therapist at the time, who was distinct in not using "they" as a pronoun and only being referred to with a proper name, I will refer to as "MTAT" for this document in order to protect MTAT's identity, who I'm presuming regrets getting involved with this fiasco and if given an option to turn back time, would have just said no to whomever was speaking to MTAT), after having been suspicious that MTAT was speaking to parties behind my back though not to any direct acknowledgement of me (though I'm also suspicious that MTAT was aware of my suspicions given changes in my own personal cadence as well as in MTAT's during the duration of my treatment led by MTAT), including a suspicion of mine that MTAT's third parties were somehow justifying watching my private sessions with MTAT (though to what measure MTAT consented to this, I am unsure), suggested that perhaps I see a psychiatrist for my issues, being vague as to why MTAT felt I was beyond their care. MTAT gave me two options - one was a referral to a private psychiatrist, another was to the Columbia Schizophrenia Research Center.

This was immediately suspicious to me - we had not spoken on schizophrenia in any way, tangential or otherwise, and it was further suspicious to me that a medical research center attached to a major university would be a plausible initial recommendation for a psychiatrist from the perspective of a psychologist. Also, surely a research center would wish for their subjects to have already been diagnosed for the condition they're researching. I've outlined elsewhere - [see the "awareness of situation" document in this same folder](00-awareness-of-situation.md) - that at this point of time, June '22, I was already suspicious of being non consensually filmed, and given that no consent or information was given to me of such a thing, was concerned I might be being framed. While this last sentence is certainly indicative of paranoia, and possibly to the point of paranoid schizophrenia, that would be the case if at the very least I _was not_ being filmed at the time.

This was my first hint that something involving Columbia and the NYSPI (which houses Columbia's Schizophrenia Research Center) was part of the non consensual filming of me.

### September or October 2022 - Parents feign Parkinson diagnosis as a way to lure me to Columbia

After already having been suspicious that my parents were roped into this theater - at whose behest I still have to find out - in order to lie to me about my nephew falling into a coma which he most assuredly had not, they contacted me again in either September or October of 2022 to let me know my father had been diagnosed with Parkinson disease. My suspicions around them lying to me about my nephew earlier in August of the same year lay in my mother's telling me of this situation. A former neurological pediatrician with a speciality in ADHD herself, whenever a medical condition would occur - even the one time I went to the ER for abdominal pain that was later likely a gas bubble - my mother, like many doctors, would discuss the ailment using medical terms that require a background in anatomy, almost reflexively. It's a tad obnoxious of her, but I've never held it against her as she does it automatically, likely because of her own medical background and having worked in a general hospital amongst peers of many different specialities. However, when relating to the coma she tried to convince me my nephew had fallen into, she seemed to be reading from a script that contained little to no anatomical or medical analysis of the situation. My father also sounded like he was faking crying on another phone call concerning my nephew. This was possibly confirmation bias on my part that I was, as I began to feel certain of in August, in some kind of weird reality TV / Hollywood thing, but I'm still waiting on someone to prove to me that that is certainly not the case, while also explaining the other oddities of my life (such as, but not limited to, not receiving email responses or text messages or phone calls pertaining to this situation).

So, when my mother let me know of father's diagnosis of Parkinson, I was already suspicious of her faking a previous medical issue with my nephew, and this time around, similar to last, she was devoid of medical / anatomical discussions, which was doubly suspicious - as a former neurologist herself, my mother would and should have had more to say about Parkinson disease, a neurologically rooted degenerative motor disease.

What cemented my suspicions was their requesting me to visit them at Columbia University - surely, for two New Jersey residents, there would be a qualified hospital to manage this degenerative condition closer to their home. This occurred near other suspicions of mine that some type of medical research was being performed in conjunction with the filming of me in my private residence, and all without my consent (see "awareness" document). They've continued to lie to me about my father having Parkinson disease - again, a progressively degenerative disease - to this day, despite that nearly 4 years after their "diagnosis", he shows no symptoms, let alone progressively deteriorating ones. If Columbia has managed his Parkinson to such a noteworthy degree, I'd say that would be more qualified as research, and would congratulate whom my mother has identified as their doctor, Dr. Cheryl Waters, of Columbia University - were any of this to be true.
