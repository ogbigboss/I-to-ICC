### Why is this necessary?

I'm worried one of the ways this has gone on for so long is those blocking my comms (see final section on awareness of digital fraud) have also been falsifying messages on my behalf, such to generate the impression that I want this to continue in any way shape or form (I do not, and would prefer it's ending to come at the hand of those that started it, but I wanted to wait until near the end of any potential SoL to take actions myself that could end things, if my presumptions are correct about some other things).

Thus, I am writing this so there is a document written before any actions I take that could end things are taken on my behalf. provided they end things, and I am left with some confirmation that at the very least filming without my consent and medical research did indeed happen, I can point to this document as my rationale towards how these things were happening despite not receiving direct communications, all dated to a period of time before said ending can occur.

That said, at the time of writing, it's still somewhat incomplete, but I tried to get the best of the information in given a constrained timeline before taking actions that could possibly end this affair.
